Offer agreement


Public offer agreement for the sale and purchase of goods
This agreement is addressed to consumers and is the official public offer of the Seller to conclude a sale and purchase agreement for goods. By acting for the purpose of purchasing goods, the consumer accepts the terms of this agreement under the conditions set out below.


1. General provisions



1.1. This agreement is a public offer agreement in accordance with Articles 633, 641, and Chapter 54 of the Civil Code of Ukraine. Its terms are the same for all Buyers regardless of status.
1.2. By accepting the terms of this agreement, that is, the Seller's public offer, the consumer becomes the Buyer.
1.3. By concluding this agreement, the Buyer confirms full acquaintance with and consent to its terms and, if the Buyer is an individual, grants consent to the processing of personal data for the purpose of fulfilling this agreement, carrying out settlements, and issuing invoices, acts, and other documents. This consent remains valid throughout the term of the agreement and for an unlimited period after its expiration.


2. Definitions



2.1. Seller means an individual or legal entity that sells goods on the website based on a license agreement for the use of the Zoo Feed trademark.
2.2. Goods mean the list of consumer products published on the online store website, including the price, name, and description, and may include product images.
2.3. Buyer means a legally capable individual who has reached the age of eighteen and places an order for goods on the website for purposes not related to business activity, or a legal entity or individual entrepreneur.
2.4. Order means the Buyer’s request for the purchase of goods, duly completed and submitted to the Seller.
2.5. Online store website means a collection of pages accessed via a web browser and software modules combined into a single management system.
2.6. Personal account means a personal section of the Seller’s website available to the Buyer, containing personal information and order history.


3. Subject of the agreement



3.1. The Seller undertakes to sell the Goods on the basis of the Order placed by the Buyer on the online store website, and the Buyer undertakes to purchase the Goods and pay their cost under the terms of this agreement.
3.2. The Seller guarantees that the Goods are not pledged, not under dispute or arrest, and are not subject to third-party rights.
3.3. The Seller and the Buyer confirm that this agreement is not fictitious, sham, or concluded under the influence of violence or fraud.
3.4. The Seller confirms that it has all necessary permits to carry out business activities related to this agreement and the right to manufacture and/or sell the Goods in accordance with Ukrainian law.
3.5. The Buyer accepts the terms of this agreement at the moment of placing the Order by clicking the “Place an order” button. Such action confirms that the Buyer has read the agreement and agrees with its terms.


4. Rules for using the online store website



4.1. The Buyer may browse the website, place orders, and use other website services.
4.2. Registration is required to access personalized information and additional website features.
4.3. During registration, the Buyer provides the data requested by the website form.
4.4. The Buyer undertakes to provide true, accurate, and complete information. The Seller is not responsible for the accuracy of information provided by the Buyer.
4.5. The Buyer undertakes not to disclose access credentials to third parties and must immediately notify the Seller in case of suspected unauthorized use.
4.6. The Buyer is responsible for all actions carried out on the website under their account and on their behalf.
4.7. The Seller has the right to refuse order processing if it believes the Buyer has provided false information or behaved in bad faith.
4.8. It is prohibited to use the online store website for unlawful activity or to post extremist, pornographic, offensive, or unauthorized advertising materials.


5. Order placement procedure



5.1. The Buyer places an Order on the website independently by adding selected Goods to the virtual cart and clicking the “Buy” button.
5.2. Goods may be ordered by both registered and unregistered Buyers, including by phone using the number listed on the website.


6. Price and payment procedure



6.1. The price of each individual product is determined by the Seller and indicated on the website. The total Order price is formed from the prices of all included Goods and the delivery price.
6.2. The agreement price equals the Order price. The total amount may change depending on the price, quantity, or assortment of Goods.
6.3. The Buyer pays for the Goods according to the Order using one of the payment methods offered on the website.
6.4. Payment is made in the national currency of Ukraine.
6.5. The Order is considered paid from the moment the funds are credited to the Seller’s account or the account of its representative.
6.6. Delivery is carried out only after full payment for the Goods.
6.7. The Seller may change the price of Goods unilaterally, but the price of an already ordered product is not subject to change.
6.8. Until the order is confirmed by a Zoo Feed manager by phone, the Goods are not reserved. After confirmation, the Goods are reserved for three business days until payment is received.


7. Return conditions



7.1. If the ordered Goods were listed at an incorrect price, the Seller informs the Buyer as soon as possible to confirm or cancel the Order. If communication with the Buyer is impossible, the Order is considered canceled automatically. If the Order was paid, the Seller returns the paid amount to the Buyer.


8. Obligations of the Seller



8.1. Provide truthful information about the Goods, their prices, and delivery terms.
8.2. Deliver the Goods according to the Order terms.
8.3. Guarantee that the quality of the Goods complies with applicable quality requirements in Ukraine and fulfill warranty obligations under this agreement.
8.4. Immediately inform the Buyer about changes in delivery terms by phone or email.


9. Obligations of the Buyer



9.1. Provide the Seller with accurate and truthful information, including during website registration.
9.2. Pay for the Goods according to the price specified in the Order.
9.3. Upon receiving the Order, inspect the packaging for visible damage and, if there is no visible damage, sign the delivery note or receipt. Open the outer packaging in the presence of the delivery representative and inspect the Goods.
9.4. If there are claims, require the delivery representative to draw up an inspection report and an acceptance report in three copies.
9.5. If the Goods are transferred in violation of the Order terms, notify the Seller of these violations no later than five calendar days after purchase or receipt.
9.6. Provide accurate passport data where necessary to receive the Order from the courier company office.


10. Rights of the Buyer



10.1. Demand a refund if the Seller is unable to fulfill the Order.
10.2. Make changes to the Order before payment.
10.3. Refuse to perform the agreement before the Goods are transferred, subject to reimbursement of the Seller’s transport costs.
10.4. Make claims regarding the quality of the Goods within the terms established by the Law of Ukraine “On Consumer Protection”.


11. Liability of the parties



11.1. The Seller is not liable for damage caused to the Buyer as a result of improper use of Goods ordered on the website.
11.2. The Seller is not liable for improper or untimely performance of Orders if the Buyer provided false or inaccurate information.
11.3. The Seller and the Buyer are liable for fulfilling their obligations under this agreement in accordance with current Ukrainian legislation.
11.4. The parties are released from liability for full or partial non-performance if such non-performance is caused by force majeure circumstances such as war, military actions, earthquake, flood, fire, acts of public authorities, changes in customs rules, or import/export restrictions.


12. Confidentiality and personal data protection



12.1. When registering on the website, the Buyer provides the personal and contact data specified in section 4.3 of this agreement.
12.2. By providing personal data during registration or order placement, the Buyer gives voluntary consent to the processing and use of such personal data without limitation of duration.
12.3. The Seller uses personal data to provide services under this agreement and to promote the services offered by the Seller.
12.4. The Seller undertakes not to disclose information received from the Buyer, except in cases required for fulfilling obligations or by law.
12.5. The Buyer is responsible for keeping personal data up to date.


13. Informational messages



13.1. By registering on the website, the Buyer agrees to receive informational messages from the Seller and its partners via SMS and email.
13.2. The Buyer may unsubscribe from such mailings at any time by sending a message through the feedback form.


14. Rules for using materials posted on the website



14.1. The online store website contains materials, trademarks, trade names, and other legally protected materials, including texts, photographs, graphics, musical, and sound works.
14.2. All content of the online store website is protected by Ukrainian law.
14.3. The Buyer has no right to use, modify, publish, transfer, sell, or create derivative works from materials posted on the website without permission.

15.1. This agreement is concluded in the territory of Ukraine and is governed by Ukrainian legislation.
15.2. The invalidity of any individual clause or part of this agreement does not invalidate the agreement as a whole.
15.3. All disputes between the Buyer and the Seller are resolved through negotiations, and if no settlement is reached, the dispute may be submitted to court in accordance with current legislation.
